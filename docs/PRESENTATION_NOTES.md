# Recording preparation

Use the seven-slide deck in `presentation/After-Hours-Presentation.pptx`. The live website and repository links are clickable on the last slide. The five-minute video is still to be recorded by Youssef. Record only after you can explain the actual code and accurately describe your contribution.

## Suggested timing and demonstration

1. **After Hours, about 30 seconds:** introduce the idea of ending a digital day. The site communicates through removal, sorting and a changing atmosphere rather than an article or portfolio. It makes no medical claim.
2. **Five moments, about 45 seconds:** open the live website. Show the arrival dial, clear a notification, and place a thought. Mention the boundary and quiet ending. Journey dots let you move directly between scenes when demonstrating.
3. **Actions reveal the experience, about 45 seconds:** explain why turning, swiping and placing fit the idea. Show the button alternative to one gesture. These are routes to the same state, not separate experiences.
4. **State connects actions to the page, about 50 seconds:** demonstrate the boundary slider. Explain that its input value changes a state property, percentage text and CSS variables. At a threshold of 100, outside words have an opacity of zero. Explain that the Set of dismissed IDs prevents double-counting.
5. **A quieter visual language, about 40 seconds:** discuss violet/lavender, generous spacing, Arial instructions and Georgia reflective phrases. Demonstrate Pause motion. Mention keyboard alternatives, focus and reduced-motion handling without claiming a professional accessibility audit.
6. **Design decisions and limitations, about 45 seconds:** explain the paused entrance-animation bug and correction. Mention fictional messages, no real notifications, no storage after reload and restarting to revise sorting choices. Explain code, writing and documentation assistance accurately.
7. **Links and references, about 25 seconds:** show both project URLs and point out implementation references and credits. Close the video within the five-minute limit.

These timings total 4 minutes 40 seconds, leaving about 20 seconds for changing tabs. Practise once to account for your speaking speed. Use short prompts if preferred; do not read text that you cannot explain.

## Before recording

- Open only the slide deck and public website in the screen/window you will share.
- Reset the website once, then check that the live version loads.
- Make a short test recording to confirm the microphone, screen and camera setup you intend to use.
- Keep the mouse visible while demonstrating a gesture.
- Do not leave the video on a loading screen, private files or a login prompt.
- Check the exported recording from beginning to end before adding it to the coursework submission.
