ABOUT AFTER HOURS

A screen that asks
for less.

After Hours is an expressive interface about the difficulty of leaving a busy digital day. Its fictional messages and changing atmosphere turn attention into something you can move, sort and release.

This is a creative experience, not a wellbeing assessment. It does not access notifications, collect personal information, change your device settings or play audio.

Created for CCT360 by Youssef Alhabbal with OpenAI Codex assistance. Typography uses your device’s system fonts. All text and visual scenes are created for this project; no external media, API or tracking is used.

Controls: use buttons or the journey dots to move between moments. Swipe messages horizontally, or dismiss them with a button. Drag thoughts into a zone, or select a thought and then a zone. Every action has a keyboard alternative. Motion can be paused.
